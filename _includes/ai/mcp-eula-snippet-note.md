The `--accepteula` argument accepts the [license terms of the Power BI Authoring MCP server](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?LinkId=2381247). Version 1.0 and later of the server require this acceptance before the first use. The argument in the snippet removes an extra step at the first start. To review and accept the license terms in the chat, remove the argument. The step that connects to Power BI Desktop shows how.
